# LABORATORY OPERATING RULES & GENERAL GUIDELINES

SECTION 1: LABORATORY OPERATING HOURS
1.1 Standard Operating Hours & Daily Booking Timeslots:
- Monday through Friday: 08:00 AM to 20:00 PM (08:00 - 20:00 / 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM).
- Weekday Afternoon Sessions: Booking slots scheduled between 4 PM to 6 PM (16:00 - 18:00) fall strictly within regular open laboratory operating hours.
- Saturday: 09:00 AM to 17:00 PM (09:00 - 17:00 / 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM).
- Sunday: CLOSED for facility deep cleaning and system maintenance.
- Official university holidays: CLOSED unless special operational exemption is granted.
- General Rule: All equipment bookings must be scheduled within standard operating hours unless an After-Hours Access Permit is approved.

1.2 After-Hours Access Policy:
- Any laboratory access between 20:00 PM and 08:00 AM on weekdays or after 17:00 PM on Saturdays is classified as "After-Hours".
- After-hours work is restricted to Level 2 (Graduate/Postgraduate) and Level 3 (Faculty/Researchers) personnel.
- Undergraduates are strictly prohibited from working in the laboratory after hours without the physical presence of an authorized supervisor.
- An After-Hours Access Permit must be submitted and approved by the Lab Safety Officer at least 24 hours in advance.
- The "Two-Person Rule" (Buddy System) applies to all after-hours laboratory work involving chemicals, high voltages, or hazardous equipment.

SECTION 2: LABORATORY SAFETY & PPE REQUIREMENTS
2.1 Mandatory Personal Protective Equipment (PPE):
- Standard lab coat (100% cotton or flame-resistant depending on chemical protocol).
- ANSI Z87.1 certified safety glasses or chemical splash goggles at all times.
- Closed-toe, non-slip footwear covering the entire foot (no sandals, crocs, or mesh shoes).
- Nitrile or neoprene gloves appropriate for handled substances. Gloves must be removed before touching door handles, computers, or mobile devices.

2.2 Cleanliness and Waste Disposal:
- Workbenches must be decontaminated with 70% ethanol or approved disinfectant before and immediately after each session.
- Chemical waste must be segregated into designated halogenated, non-halogenated, aqueous, or heavy metal waste containers.
- Broken glassware and sharps must strictly be placed in puncture-resistant Yellow Sharps Bins.
- Sinks and eyewash stations must remain unobstructed at all times.

SECTION 3: EMERGENCY PROTOCOLS & ACCIDENT REPORTING
3.1 Chemical Spills:
- Minor spills (< 100 mL of low toxicity): Use workbench spill kit, neutralize, and notify lab technician within 1 hour.
- Major spills (> 100 mL or toxic fumes): Evacuate the room immediately, pull fire alarm if necessary, and call emergency extension 5555.
3.2 Incident Logging:
- All accidents, near-misses, and equipment malfunctions must be formally logged in the Laboratory Incident Register within 24 hours.
